Rip Them Off - Privacy Policy

Last modification date : October 27, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains our policy regarding the collection, use, sharing, and retention of your information when you download, access, and/or use Rip Them Off. These terms also apply to any related services such as customer support, social media, and community channels. By using our app, you consent to the information practices described in this policy.

1. How Data is Captured (Collection)

We do not operate our own dedicated user databases or servers to actively profile or collect direct personal information (such as your name, email, or address) from gameplay. However, data is captured in the following ways:

  • Game Progress and Save Data: If you enable cloud save features (such as Apple's iCloud service), your game progress, settings, and save files are automatically captured and synchronized across your authorized devices.
  • Platform and Storefront Data: When you purchase or download the game through digital distribution platforms (such as the Epic Games Store, Steam, Nintendo eShop, PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Google Play, or the App Store), those third-party platforms collect user and transactional data in accordance with their own respective privacy policies.

2. How Data is Used

We use the limited data associated with your game experience for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and support game functionality, including the synchronization of your game saves across devices via cloud services.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries or technical issues you send directly to our team.

3. How Data is Shared

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data sharing is strictly limited to:

  • Cloud Service Providers: If you utilize cloud saving features, your save files are transmitted directly between your device and the cloud provider (e.g., Apple iCloud) to facilitate cross-device syncing.
  • Platform Partners: Digital storefronts and platforms through which you acquire the game may process diagnostic, purchase, or telemetry data according to their agreements with you.

4. How Data is Retained

Because we do not store personal player data on our own servers, we do not retain user databases.

  • Cloud Saves: Game save data stored via cloud providers (such as iCloud) is retained by the respective platform provider for as long as you maintain your account and use the cloud service. You can delete or manage this data directly through your device or platform account settings.
  • Communications: If you contact us via email for support, we retain your email correspondence only as long as necessary to resolve your request.

Children’s Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that data concerning a child has been inadvertently collected via platform services, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

Your Consent and Choices

By playing Rip Them Off, you agree to this Privacy Policy. You can disable features like cloud saves at any time through your device or platform settings if you prefer local-only data storage.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page and update the modification date above. We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information.

Contacting Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us at: support@lozange-lab.com
